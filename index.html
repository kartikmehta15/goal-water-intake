📖 Gmail SMTP Setup Guide (5 minutes)

Step 1: Enable 2-Step Verification

  1. Visit Google Account Security
  2. Find "2-Step Verification" and enable it
  3. Complete the verification process

Step 2: Generate App Password

  1. Go to App Passwords
  2. Select app: Mail
  3. Select device: Other (name it "Water Tracker")
  4. Click Generate
  5. Copy the 16-character password (remove spaces)

Step 3: Configure Above

  • SMTP Host: smtp.gmail.com
  • SMTP Port: 587
  • Email: your-email@gmail.com
  • Password: Paste the 16-character App Password

Step 4: Test

Click "Save & Test Email" to verify your configuration. You should receive a test email within seconds.

✅ Free Forever: Gmail allows 500 emails/day for free (no credit card needed)
